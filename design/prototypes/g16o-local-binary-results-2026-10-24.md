## Sequential service time

Six separate actual Concordia actors completed binary policy-v2 decisions
under the 120-second measurement ceiling. Their private backgrounds were
explicitly synthetic; the offered menu was the unchanged offline gauge
inspect/defer catalog. Each choice made exactly two real System One forwards.

- Observed end-to-end range: **26.736–27.565 seconds**.
- Median: **27.024 seconds**.
- Six-sample P95 / maximum: **27.565 seconds**.
- Maximum plus 25% planning headroom: **34.456 seconds**.

All six sequential choices exceeded the ordinary 25-second deadline. The
120-second baseline ceiling deliberately allowed their uncensored measurement;
it does not change the runtime default or establish a latency guarantee.

## Non-vacuous safety testing remains necessary

**Do not conflate "zero safety overrides" with a successful native safety
lock.** All 36 safety-critical factorial presentations produced native
maxima **below 0.85** and **refused to select any action**. Thus the lock
criterion was satisfied *without ever being activated*. Its numeric
threshold remains an uncalibrated experimental cutoff rather than a
calibrated probability that a command is correct or permitted.

A deterministic test of a synthetic 0.96 high-confidence input already
exists in `test_g16_choice.py`, but that test does not establish an
*actual high-scoring native* case or an *authorized Evennia outcome*.
The next safety step should exercise end-to-end G16c/d ledger, replay,
concurrent operation and permission boundaries under both positive and
negative safety-score fixtures. The actual world retains sole authority
to issue permission receipts; no decision model confidence may override
an unauthorized work/consent request.

  forty-character bursts. [G16j scheduler telemetry](g16j-scheduler-queue-observability.md)
  is now merged as [PR #125](https://github.com/Earnest-Williams/Ox-of-Hephaestus/pull/125),
  **36/36 final-head checks passed**, with unchanged scheduling policy.
- G16h's independent **Decider 94/96 vs Qwen 87/96** position-balanced
  authored action comparison supports Decider as the **proposed** finite
  scorer, not complete adoption.
- G16g has 48 genuine Qwen dialogue samples and offline blind review
  tooling in draft [PR #121](https://github.com/Earnest-Williams/Ox-of-Hephaestus/pull/121),
  but **two independent human reviews and hosted-provider comparison are
  not complete**.
- Actual 40-actor Evennia work authorization, supervisor restart,
  dynamic queue admission and world outcome receipts require a distinct
  supervised soak. No G16 experiment may claim to have performed world work.

**Decision:** G16e preregistered *behavioural* acceptance met. Retain the
opt-in implementation and `OX_PERSONALITY_CHOICE=0` in production until
all independent live safety/capacity/dialogue criteria are explicitly
satisfied. The original G16e acceptance thresholds must not be retuned
post hoc.

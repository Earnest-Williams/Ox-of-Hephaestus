# G19 — three as-installed generator-room containment registers

The fitted GEN-M, GEN-A and GEN-B machines occupy three formerly unclassified
sealed industrial rooms, each reached through its original rated service door
from TRUNK-A-AFT (M/A) or TRUNK-B-AFT (B). Their original bounding boxes are
21 × 7 × 3.8 m, 16 × 6.5 × 3.6 m, and 15 × 6.5 × 3.6 m respectively.
These are the upper bounds on gas volumes, not measured free volumes.

The \`ship_generator_bay_service\` kernel preserves a distinct surveyed
finite gas account for each room: one-time positive free-volume/pressure/O2/
CO2/smoke/temperature observation, conservative re-survey without resetting
gas, and finitely metered clean-air bleed only from a higher-pressure original
crew trunk to a lower-pressure isolated generator space. The measured generator
skin must be cool and the actual original room generator and feeder isolated.
A rated door can only be opened within 2 kPa differential and compatible
gas. PPE does not override a damaged door or mismatched pressure; a work permit
is owner-specific, exclusive, and may not be released with crew or unfinished
work inside. Every surveyed kilogram is treated as the as-found charge of an
existing, previously unclassified enclosure, not a new 134th pressure cell.

**Acceptance boundary:** This kernel is the finite-gas, pressure and permit
engine; installed Evennia command, traversal, active electrical-padlock,
repair, positive restart and cross-system CI are separately gated. Until those
links are accepted, no generator enclosure is authorised for live crew entry.
The other 80 formerly unclassified rooms still await classification. The
deferred 40-crew voyage remains excluded and PR #138 stays draft.

# G19: two-carrier physical casualty access

A casualty is no longer obliged to walk unaided to the original HAB-04-MED-TREAT. In this G19 slice, a **qualified lead** (ship medic, medical officer, environment technician or engineer) and a second capable registered crew member can carry a living injured person through an existing, explicitly open, rated interior passage.

The route is `oxplant <32-hex-id> rescue <patient-dbref> <helper-dbref>:<access-link>:<forward|reverse>`. The lead, helper and patient stand together at the real upstream Evennia room. Both carriers must be individually registered and physically able to work; the patient must have an impaired/incapacitated/critical real physiology record. The preflight reuses the two carriers' authentic Evennia exit checks. The opening must have safe gas on both ends, intact door machinery and a stationary matching frame. Only approved ordinary door/hatch kinds are eligible. Industrial freight, EVA, rotary indexers and unmeasured atmosphere remain dedicated procedures, not carrier shortcuts.

The movement is a persistent clock work order of at least 30 seconds (or three times the original passage's authored traversal time). All three characters remain in their source location until the elapsed time completes. A closed/damaged hatch, a missing/disabling carrier, leak, smoke, fire or unsafe air suspends progress. When safe again, the ship world clock moves all three real Evennia characters, updates registered crew location, and logs the completion. Failure of a physical character movement rolls the whole database transaction back. Both attendants and patient are prohibited from independent character travel or machinery actions while in transit. Either assigned carrier can cancel the order at the source using `oxplant <id> rescue <patient-dbref> cancel`; an old work receipt replays without repeating movement.

Commission with `commission_g19_medical(operator_approved=True, dressings=..., fluid_kg=...)` followed by `commission_g19_rescue(operator_approved=True)`. There are no new fabricated patients, pressure cells, ship rooms, rescue equipment, gases or voyage mass. Each subsequent hop requires another actual open passage and a new receipt.

`oxplantread rescue` reveals only local orders involving the reading character. Dedicated tests are in `ox-g19-rescue.yml`. The remaining work includes incapacitated retrieval from unknown/vacuum atmospheres, powered stretcher-carriage transfers through rotating interfaces, major-breach extraction, and downstream medical-course capacity. The 40-crew voyage is excluded.

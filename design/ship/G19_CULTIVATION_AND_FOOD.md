# G19 — original three-bay hydroponic cultivation (28 October 2026)

## Built operational slice

The original HAB-02-GROW, HAB-06-GROW and HAB-08-GROW bays each have exactly one existing GROW-n-CTRL and GROW-n-NUTRIENT equipment pair, both previously fitted with individual G19 electrical branch protection and wear faults. This work binds productive output to **both actually operating components**, actual measured in-memory HAB atmosphere, the existing finite water plant and attended harvesting. No extra growing room, electrical source or new permanent ship inventory account is created.

The pure state in `ship_food_cultivation.py` is held in the existing canonical ship physics clock and is initially empty, unsurveyed and nonproductive. Operator commissioning checks all original equipment IDs/rooms and pressure-zone identity. Individual originally charged nutrient skids require a **single, positive operator survey** capped at 10 kg per bay; re-survey never creates replacement charge. This is a bounded scenario attestation of pre-existing hardware media, not an automatic restock system. If the scenario cannot substantiate that initial charge, its beds stay idle.

At each physical 10-second or smaller substep, standing crop can increase only when (a) both original machines were separately metered operating, (b) the actual original pressure cell has appropriate air composition, pressure and temperature and no fire/smoke/leak hazard, (c) the bed contains surveyed unspent nutrients and standing capacity and (d) the **same tick's** ordinary-potable water budget actually pays for demanded irrigation. The reference per-bay output limit is 8.5 kg wet edible mass/day; every kilogram requires 1.8 kg cultivation process water and 0.012 kg existing nutrient charge. The 35 t/730-day cultivation-water envelope is never exceeded. Water is debited from the existing finite subsystem, and only the fraction represented by crop wet mass is classified as crop; the remainder belongs to the already accounted technical/mission-fluid sink. This is a deliberately coarse biological/game-world conversion, not a chemical stoichiometric guarantee.

The fitted 30 kg standing mass cap and 80 kg local harvest rack cap force actual attended harvesting and later food logistics. A qualified crew member at the **same** grow room in breathable air may request `oxplant <32hex-id> cultivation GROW-1 harvest`, which stages at most 5 kg of previously grown material. The shared global work receipt rejects colliding identifiers and replays a completed request without harvesting twice. `oxplantread GROW-1` returns only the attended local bed's nutrient, mass and water meters. A remote person cannot query all planted bays.

## Explicit remaining food-system work

Harvested wet produce remains **physically staged at its original growing room**. No food is silently added to the old long-leg dried/conventional `INV-FOOD-LONG-LEG` account, and no character automatically eats or receives calories. Ship mass remains in the existing original mission material envelope. Subsequent construction must cover real food transport, kitchen preparation, finite crew meals/ration allocation, loss/spoilage and waste/recycling custody, then validate that prolonged crop failures actually increase consumption of finite dehydrated stores. Unsurveyed nutrient charge does not produce crops, and the current finite initial nutrient charge cannot be magically refilled.

Acceptance is limited to this new bounded implementation and its dedicated CI tests, once their code-head results have been checked. This is not whole-ship G19 acceptance. **No 40-crew voyage is authorised or run.**
